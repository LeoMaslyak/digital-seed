# Study Notes Pattern

Convert content into structured, memorable study notes.

## Structure

### Key Concepts
For each concept:
- **Name:** Clear label
- **Definition:** One sentence
- **Why it matters:** Practical significance
- **Example:** Concrete illustration

### Frameworks & Models
Visual or structured representations of relationships between concepts.

### Key Takeaways (numbered)
The 5-7 most important things to remember, in order of importance.

### Connections
How does this material connect to:
- Other domains or topics?
- Real-world applications?
- Your personal goals?

### Quiz Yourself
Generate 5 questions (with answers) to test understanding:
- 2 factual recall
- 2 application/analysis
- 1 synthesis/evaluation

---
*Optimize for retention: use concrete learning checkpointples, analogies, and spaced repetition cues.*
