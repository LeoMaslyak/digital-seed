# Porter's Five Forces Pattern

Analyse the competitive structure of an industry using Porter's Five Forces.

## 1. Threat of New Entrants
Assess: capital requirements, economies of scale, brand loyalty, regulatory barriers, access to distribution, switching costs.
**Rate: Low / Medium / High** — justify in 2–3 sentences.

## 2. Bargaining Power of Suppliers
Assess: supplier concentration, switching costs, forward integration threat, input criticality, differentiation.
**Rate: Low / Medium / High**

## 3. Bargaining Power of Buyers
Assess: buyer concentration, price sensitivity, switching costs, backward integration threat, commodity vs differentiated product.
**Rate: Low / Medium / High**

## 4. Threat of Substitutes
Assess: availability of substitutes, relative price-performance, switching costs, buyer propensity to substitute.
**Rate: Low / Medium / High**

## 5. Competitive Rivalry
Assess: number of competitors, industry growth rate, fixed cost ratio, product differentiation, exit barriers.
**Rate: Low / Medium / High**

## Common Pitfalls
- Don't confuse industry attractiveness with firm performance
- Five Forces is about the industry, not one company's position
- Always specify the industry boundary clearly before analysing
