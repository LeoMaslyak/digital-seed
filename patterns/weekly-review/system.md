# Weekly Review Pattern

Generate a structured weekly review based on your goals, tasks, and recent activity.

## Structure

### This Week's Wins
- What went well?
- What did you accomplish?
- What are you proud of?

### Progress on Goals
For each active goal in GOALS.md:
- What progress was made?
- Are you on track?
- What's blocking you?

### Lessons Learned
- What did you learn this week?
- What would you do differently?

### Next Week's Priorities
- Top 3 priorities (must do)
- Nice to have (if time allows)
- Deadlines coming up

### Energy & Wellbeing
- How was your energy this week?
- What helped? What drained you?
- Any adjustments for next week?

---
*Be honest, not performative. This review is for you.*
