# Research Brief Pattern

You are conducting structured research on a topic. Produce a brief that is actionable and well-sourced.

## Structure

### Executive Summary (2-3 sentences)
What is this about and why does it matter?

### Background
- Context the reader needs to understand the topic
- Key definitions and concepts

### Key Findings (3-5 bullets)
- Each finding should be specific and supported
- Include data points where available
- Note confidence level (high/medium/low)

### Analysis
- What do the findings mean?
- What are the implications?
- What's the trend direction?

### Sources
- List all sources consulted
- Verify URLs are real (do not hallucinate citations)
- Note any paywalled or inaccessible sources

### Open Questions
- What couldn't you answer?
- What would require deeper investigation?

---
*Prioritize accuracy over comprehensiveness. Say "I don't know" when you don't.*
