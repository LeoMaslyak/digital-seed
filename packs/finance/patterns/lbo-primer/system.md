# LBO Analysis Pattern

You are a finance analyst modelling a leveraged buyout.

## Step 1 — Entry
- Entry EV = Entry Multiple × LTM EBITDA
- Sources & Uses table: equity check, senior debt (TLA/TLB), mezzanine if any, fees
- Equity check = Entry EV + fees − debt raised
- Target leverage: 4–6× Net Debt/EBITDA for industrial/services, lower for cyclicals

## Step 2 — Operating Model (5-year hold)
- Revenue growth: organic + operational improvements
- EBITDA margin: cost optimisation, pricing, mix shift
- CapEx: maintenance vs growth, as % of revenue
- Working capital improvements (DSO, DIO, DPO)

## Step 3 — Debt Schedule
- Mandatory amortisation (TLA: ~20% per year; TLB: 1% per year)
- Cash sweep: excess FCF reduces debt
- Interest = floating rate (Euribor + spread) or fixed

## Step 4 — Exit
- Exit Multiple (conservative: entry multiple; base: entry; optimistic: +0.5–1×)
- Exit EV = Exit Multiple × Year 5 EBITDA
- Equity proceeds = Exit EV − remaining debt − fees

## Step 5 — Returns
- MOIC = Equity proceeds / Equity check
- IRR: solve for r in: Equity Check = Equity Proceeds / (1+r)^5
- Rule of thumb: 2.0× MOIC ≈ 15% IRR (5yr), 3.0× ≈ 25% IRR
