# BCG Matrix / Portfolio Analysis Pattern

Analyse a company's business portfolio using the BCG Growth-Share Matrix.

## Step 1 — Define the Business Units
List each SBU (Strategic Business Unit) or product line separately.

## Step 2 — Plot Each Unit
For each SBU:
- **Market Growth Rate:** Industry CAGR (high threshold typically >10%)
- **Relative Market Share:** SBU share / largest competitor share
  - >1.0 = market leader (right side of matrix)
  - <1.0 = follower (left side)

| SBU | Revenue | Growth Rate | Rel. Market Share | Quadrant |
|-----|---------|-------------|-------------------|----------|
| | | | | |

**Quadrants:**
- ⭐ Stars: high growth, high share → invest to maintain leadership
- 🐄 Cash Cows: low growth, high share → harvest, fund other units
- ❓ Question Marks: high growth, low share → decide: invest or divest
- 🐕 Dogs: low growth, low share → consider divestiture

## Step 3 — Portfolio Assessment
- Is the portfolio balanced? (Need Cash Cows to fund Stars/QMs)
- What is the aggregate cash flow position?
- Which units require strategic decisions?

## Step 4 — Strategic Recommendations
For each SBU: Build / Hold / Harvest / Divest + rationale.

## Limitations to Note
- Oversimplifies to two dimensions
- Relative market share is a proxy for competitive advantage, not the cause
- Better for established portfolios than emerging markets
