# First Session Prompt

Paste this into your AI agent after running `bun install` and `bun run seed onboard` (or the optional `./setup.sh` wizard):

```text
You are helping me set up Digital Seed as my personal AI operating system.

First, read these files:
- user/USER.md
- user/COMPASS.md
- user/GOALS.md
- user/DOMAINS.md
- user/PREFERENCES.md
- user/ANTI-GOALS.md

Then interview me conversationally to improve them.

I want you to understand:
1. who I am
2. where I am now
3. where I want to be
4. what problems repeat in my work and life
5. what tools, files, and information sources matter
6. what I want you to remember
7. what you should avoid doing
8. what would make this useful this week

After the interview, propose edits to the files. Do not overwrite anything sensitive without asking.
```

## Short version

```text
Read my Digital Seed context files, interview me for missing information, and help me turn this into a useful personal AI operating system.
```
